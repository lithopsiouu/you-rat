A puzzle game about rats stopping the end of the world.
Scientists discover the world-ending zombie virus does not affect rats. they
begin conducting mass experiments on them using puzzles staged as real-life
homes and scenarios in an effort to train the rats to deliver antidotes
effectively. 
The only problem is that the world's scientists had nearly experimented the
mouse population to extinction, so the United Recovery Front (URF) appointed
two rats to the last remaining continents. But there werent enough pairs to go
around. The player's continent only gets one mouse, and a slew of tests
to solve before humanity is entirely engulfed by rot.
